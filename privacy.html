Privacy Policy Last Updated: April 28, 2026 Introduction Clawnb (“we” or the “App”) is the mobile client of OpenClaw, a self-hosted personal AI assistant platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use Clawnb. Data Collection Clawnb uses a local-first architecture. We do not operate servers that store your data. Your conversations, configurations, and agent data are stored locally on your device and in a self-hosted OpenClaw gateway. No Account Required. Clawnb does not require you to create an account or provide personal information. No Analytics or Tracking. We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data. No Data Sharing with Third Parties. We do not share any data with third parties. Data Stored on Your Device Clawnb stores the following data locally on your device: Gateway connection settings (server address, authentication token) Chat message cache for offline access App preferences (theme, display settings) Relay pairing token (stored in the system security keychain) Network Communication Clawnb connects directly to your self-hosted OpenClaw gateway. All communication occurs between your device and the gateway. When using relay mode, data is transmitted through an encrypted tunnel; we do not inspect, store, or log relay traffic. Device Permissions Clawnb may request the following device permissions: Camera: Used to scan QR codes during relay pairing. Photo Gallery: Used to save exported statistical posters and send images in chat. Location: Optional, used only if you choose to share location context with the AI ​​agent. All permissions are optional and requested only when using the relevant functionality. Children's Privacy Clawnb is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Changes to this Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the latest revision date.
